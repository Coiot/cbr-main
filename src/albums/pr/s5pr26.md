It was a pretty brilliant play, all things considered - the Yanomami didn’t even have Galleons, and so Xaragua held a decisive advantage that they immediately pressed. One or two civs that are playing a completely different game adds a bit of spice, and Xaragua at least had the guts to try a couple of invasions even if they fell flat. They played poorly in the beginning and got unlucky after that, but they were still a goofy little science turtle, and maybe we need that in this game. I still would’ve preferred the Caribbean empire though. F, may your birds share many kisses in the sub.</p>"
